## Good Mentions

- Strong modularization is already in place: reusable UI primitives are centralized under `src/components/ui/`.
- Navigation flow is clear: top-level routing in `src/App.jsx` and sidebar-driven route actions in `src/components/SideBar.jsx`.
- Form strategy is improving: `react-hook-form` + `zod` in payment details is a solid validation pattern to replicate for auth/wallet forms.
- The workflow has a good progressive structure: scaffold pages first, then attach business interactions (dialogs, forms, navigation).
